Automation triggers and workflow scripts (D-019, DOMAIN.md 13.8). Inbound hooks: a webhook trigger kind with an unguessable endpoint, a signing secret held by the secret broker and shown once, HMAC over timestamp and body with a tolerance window, a durable replay ledger (delivery id and signature digest), a 64 KiB cap, a per-endpoint rate limit, typed filters (OR of groups, AND within a group), fires claimed in the existing fire ledger before execution, and the payload reaching the run only as an UNTRUSTED_EXTERNAL block through the INT-012 segment path. Management: expected_revision with full-replacement semantics and a typed conflict, idempotent creates by client request id, per-plan limits from config/automation.yaml, a rate-limited dry run, copy that says a trigger or script is the tool for waiting. IANA time zones (chrono-tz) with explicit gap and repeat rules and a typed error for an unknown name. Workflow scripts: a typed IR (agent, parallel, reduce, log) with a hard max_agent_calls, compiled through the Planner to WorkGraph nodes, journalled as Evidence on the script run (no new store), resumed after a restart without repeating a finished call, gated through the existing approval path above 20 calls, paused into an attention item at the budget, with schema-validated item results. Desktop: hook entries (endpoint and secret shown once), dry run, revision-conflict handling, an IANA picker and a script view with its budget. Recovery proven with the real quansio-server process killed with SIGKILL and relaunched. Not exercised live (evidence NOTES.md): a real model, Electron/browser clicks for the new UI, two served processes racing for one script. Deferred: connector-event triggers (no event source exists), desktop authoring of a script, per-item status in the script view, automatic retry of a failed worker call.
